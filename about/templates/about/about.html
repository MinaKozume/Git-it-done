About KAFEI

KAFEI is a Mauritian-born coffee shop inspired by warm moments, slow mornings, and handcrafted flavors.

We blend premium beans, artisanal methods, and a cozy atmosphere to create an experience—not just a drink.

☕ Our Story

Started by a small team of coffee lovers, KAFEI was built on the idea that every cup should feel personal.

From handpicked beans to freshly brewed cups, every detail reflects passion.
